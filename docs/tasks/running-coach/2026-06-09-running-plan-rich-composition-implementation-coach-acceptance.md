## Accepted Historical Scope

Running Coach accepted the former deterministic rich-composition matrix for create-path architecture
planning. The reviewed matrix contained 468 scenarios: 247 preview-ready and 221 unavailable. At
that time it showed sufficient differentiation among 10K, Half Marathon, and Marathon Base outputs
to move beyond isolated matrix refinement.

That acceptance was bounded:

- it did not claim perfect training richness;
- it did not approve persistence or runner-facing rollout;
- its unavailable reasons predated the later no-dead-end work;
- `Marathon Base` was accepted only as the old base-building family, not marathon race readiness.

## Durable Acceptance Lessons

- Technical validity is insufficient when the visible plan is flat.
- Conservative plans must preserve goal meaning through softer training signals.
- Long-run roles, recovery placement, cutbacks, and endpoint honesty must remain visible.
- Workout richness must not be manufactured through unsafe intensity or unsupported targets.
